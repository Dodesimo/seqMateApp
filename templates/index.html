SeqMate

Automating the RNA Sequencing pipeline.

Our product allows any researcher to almost fully automate their RNA-Seq workflow. Using this product, you can input the raw output of any transcriptome sequencing experiments. The pipeline then returns a complete write-up of differential expression analysis, including automatically generated BAM & SAM files, count matrices, a full-page workup on any differentially expressed genes (with cutoffs designated by the user), and various graphs and charts that visualize the textual and numerical output.
